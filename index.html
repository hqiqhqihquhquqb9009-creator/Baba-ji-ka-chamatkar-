Baba Ji Ka Chamatkar Game Kya Hai?

Baba Ji Ka Chamatkar ek free online mind reading number game hai. Isme aap man me koi bhi number sochte hain aur ye game simple math steps ke through aapka final answer bata deta hai. Ye koi jaadu nahi balki maths ka khel hai jo algebra ke basic formula pe kaam karta hai.

Kaise Khele?

1. Man me ek number sochiye
2. Game ke bataye steps follow kariye: Double karo, add karo, divide karo
3. End me apna original number minus karo
4. Game aapka answer bata dega
